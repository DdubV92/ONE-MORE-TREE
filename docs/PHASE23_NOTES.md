# Phase 23: dynamic weather, storms and storm terrors

**Origin.** Pack-21 PROMPT 21 ("Dynamic weather, storms and Terrors"), the same text as the uploaded *Phase 21 Dynamic Weather, Storms and Terrors* prompt. It is built as Phase 23 because Phases 21 and 22 were used for owner requests (payroll; longer workdays and CampOS).

**Systems affected:**
- New: `StormSchedule`, `StormTerrors`, `glass_rain.gdshader`, `data/weather/profiles.json` and `data/terror/storm_beats.json` (both generated by `tools/content/*.py`).
- Extended: `WeatherService`, `EnvironmentController`, `TerrorDirector` (reservations), `TerrorActor` (stand/stride/cue), `TerrorPlacement` (sees through the chain-link fence), `Foreman` (masked hearing, sight × visibility), `CameraWall` (CAM_11 dock & shore, relay at camera), `RecordingPlayer` (flashes in replays), `FeedService` (dynamic units), the tree-wind, ocean and terrain shaders, `BuildKit` (wet materials), saves (schema 15).

**Visits.** Each visit runs **approach → onset → build → peak → recovery**. Every parameter has its own envelope, so the cloud arrives before the rain, gusts lead the rain band, lightning only builds after the rain is heavy, and the sea calms last. A visit is cells and bands, not one global value: thunderstorms have a moving cell (lightning centre), showers have rain bands, squalls a gust front.

**Schedule (`StormSchedule`).** A pure function of the weather seed (the world seed, so every campaign on this island shares one storm calendar; the seed is saved), so a save never stores the forecast and a reload re-derives it exactly.
- Day 1 is always fair. Ordinary thunder is met by day 8, before any severe coastal storm (which needs a prior thunderstorm and day 9+).
- Major storms keep a gap from the previous one of the same profile (squall and thunderstorm 2 days, severe coastal 6 days).
- The **first major storm is quiet** (weather only, no terrors). After that, 40% of major storms are quiet.
- 35% of visits wait for nightfall; the night timeline covers 1,200 awake seconds.
- **Night-storm guarantee (added this phase):** every campaign gets at least one non-quiet thunderstorm that peaks after nightfall, from day 6. If the seed had none, the earliest eligible thunderstorm (never the first major storm) moves to the night on its own day, or a new one is added on a day with no major storm within two days. Tested over 200 seeds: 0 without one. Without this, about 4 seeds in 8 never met the Thunder Walker.
- A storm still running at bedtime leaves the next morning in its recovery.
- Hunt days run the hunt cycle on the same schedule.

**Lightning.** Strikes are records (`id, t, kind near/mid/far, offset, distance, power, strokes`), derived from the visit, about 3.2 per minute at full lightning, with up to 28% near at peak. Each strike fires once (`fired` ids are saved).
- **Thunder:** each listener hears the thunder after `clamp(distance / 343 m/s, 0.05, 9 s)`. Listeners are the player and every camera microphone currently shown (four on the quad view). Each gets its own arrival, occlusion and level.
- **Pending thunder is saved:** a save made between a flash and its thunder still plays the thunder after loading.

**Continuity.** Weather time pauses with the game. A loaded save resumes the same visit and phase with the same wetness. Preview overrides blend in over 4 seconds and are never saved.

## 2. Presentation and spatial audio

**Sky and land:**
- Cloud, fog density × up to 2.8 as visibility drops.
- Gust fronts travel through the tree-wind shader (`storm_gust` global).
- The sea gains up to +90% amplitude and more whitecaps (`sea_state`).

**Wet world:**
- Puddles with rain rings in hollows of the terrain.
- Rain beads and runnels on every exterior window (`glass_rain`). The bathroom mirror, the shower screen and the dock cup stay dry.
- Wetness rises over about 70 s of steady rain and dries over about 300 s.

**Flashes.** Near, mid and far flashes have their own envelopes; near strikes also light a pooled local light. The existing **Lightning / bright flashes** setting (full / reduced, the default / off) applies to every storm flash. With reduced or off, an encounter's flash reveal becomes a soft rim cue on the figure, so nothing depends on seeing a bright flash.

**Rain on the burn pit (owner request).** The open fire gets wet:
- **Soaking:** heat drops by (rain − 0.06) / 150 per second and recovers within about 90 s once dry.
- **Smouldering** (heat below 0.6): more smoke than flame, dimmer coals and light, slower burning (25–100% of the normal rate), and rain hissing on the coals. Eight minutes of drizzle leave it smouldering.
- **Out:** steady rain puts it out in about 3¾ minutes, a severe storm in about 2½. A plume of steam follows, then cold coals. Nothing burns while it is out: the logs wait in the pit and on the loading pile, and contract delivery is untouched (pieces count once accepted).
- **Relighting:**
  - You, at the pit, once the rain is below 0.3.
  - The crew, after 2 dry minutes in the workday.
  - The crew in the morning, if it went out overnight (no overnight burning then).
- **Saved:** heat, out and the dry timer are kept with the timber state.

**Listening layers.** The ear knows where it is: open, canopy, shore, porch, camper, cabin or vehicle. Rain on the roof, canopy drip (only with standing trees overhead: a cleared forest floor is open ground), runoff from the nearest eave and gust one-shots change with the layer.

**Masking model.** A single dB model used by the mix, the AI and the encounters:
- Thunder at its source: near 118, mid 110, far 102 dB, with a rolling envelope.
- Heavy rain bed: 46 dB.
- `masking_db(listener, t)` gives the masking at any point and time. `masking_window(strike, listener, need_db)` gives the seconds during which a sound of a given loudness is covered.
- **The Foreman uses it.** Its hearing radius shrinks under thunder (measured: 30 m → 4.5 m at the peak of a near roll) and its sight scales with visibility (× 0.5 at the worst). It ignores its own steps.
- **Night safety is unchanged:** `SafetyService.query()` stays authoritative; lightning and emissives never grant safety.

## 3. The Thunder Walker and storm evidence

The Thunder Walker is a variant of family 5 (*The Faceless Surveyor*) for night storms in their build or peak.
- **Reservation.** It is staged only through a director reservation (`TerrorDirector.reserve`). A storm encounter and an ordinary night event can never overlap, and a 75 s gap follows each storm encounter.
- **Route.** Waypoints must be grounded, reachable, off the camp's safe area and visible from the listener. On cleared land it falls back to stump corridors (tested with every tree within 110 m removed).
- **Movement.** It moves only inside masking windows at the listener: it is revealed by a flash and moves under the thunder that follows. While anyone watches it, it stands still. It leaves unseen.
  - Measured in the test: 40 m moved, 0.0 m outside windows, 4 windows, 4 reveals, all 52 steps masked at the player.
- **Save/load mid-encounter:** the same figure stands exactly where it was, and its reservation is held.
- **Witnesses:** the player, shown cameras (relayed audio through the camera microphone), workers (stimulus) and the dog each judge from their own sight and masked hearing.

## Short native playtest route (about 20 minutes)

Use the Windows build. Turn on headphones: most of this phase is heard.

1. **Subtle rain.** Copy `prepared_saves/slot_2_storm_subtle_rain.json` to slot 2 and load it.
   - Stand in the open camp: within a minute, drizzle starts and the distance softens.
   - Walk under the camper's awning and inside: the rain moves to the roof.
   - Look at a window from inside: beads form and run down.
2. **Severe storm.** Load `slot_2_storm_severe.json`.
   - Walk to the dock: the sea builds and the treeline bends in gusts that travel.
   - Count flash → thunder (about 3 s per km); a near strike lights the ground.
   - Watch the puddles fill with rings.
   - Open Settings → Lightning / bright flashes → *reduced*, then *off*: flashes soften, and nothing needed disappears.
3. **Thunder Walker.** Load `slot_2_storm_walker.json` at night in a storm.
   - Face the treeline and wait for flashes: the figure appears, then moves only under thunder. It stands still while you watch it.
   - Save in the middle, quit, reload: it is exactly where it stood.
   - Sit at the camper desk with the cameras on: thunder reaches each shown camera at its own delay.
4. **Day 36 rescue (optional).** Load `slot_2_extraction_day35.json` and survive the last minute: the rescue ship comes at dawn whatever the weather. CI also played this save under a forced severe coastal storm and the rescue still arrived. The game has no way to force a storm on a loaded save, so this check is CI-only.
5. **Review stations.** From the title screen: Preview tour → *Phase 23* row → try each station (all preview-only, never saved).

Report anything that sounds wrong (thunder arriving before the flash, rain on the roof while outdoors, a figure that moves while watched), anything that looks wrong, and any frame drops during heavy storms.
