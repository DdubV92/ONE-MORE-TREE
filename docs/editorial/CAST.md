# Crew cast sheet (Phase 17)

This sheet is anchored to the real starting roster (`scripts/economy/payroll.gd`) and to its traits. The traits have real gameplay effects, so the lines must agree with them.

- **The boss** is the player. The workers call them "boss".
- **The company** is the island-clearing employer. Workers just say "the company". The internet names the contractor *Grieve Land Services*, working for the owner *Ardent Vale Holdings*. Workers rarely use either name.
- **Biscuit** is the camp dog: friendly, food-motivated, never harmed.
- **Places:** the camper (the boss's), the cabin (crew beds, kitchen, arcade, bathroom), the yard (receiving cradle, log decks, fire pile), the burn pit, the dock, the trucks, the parcel locker and the tool rack.

## EMP-01 Marta Kell: Hard-working (legacy saves add Sociable)

- **Age and background:** early 40s. Twenty years of contract felling on the mainland, and the crew's informal lead hand.
- **Voice:**
  - Brisk, practical and dry.
  - Counts things: trees, logs, hours.
  - Uses short sentences and the occasional old-hand saying ("Wood doesn't cut itself.").
  - Calls the boss "boss", and "chief" when she's teasing.
- **Habits:** Talks about "the next one", lanes, hinge wood and sharpening. Proud of clean stumps. Hates waiting on the yard. Tracks the day's count out loud.
- **Relationships:**
  - **Dale:** old friends, competitive about output.
  - **Priya:** mentors her, respects her eye.
  - **Tomas:** protective, a bit impatient with his nerves.
  - **Joan:** easy banter; Joan teases her about never stopping.
- **Speaks when:** work is flowing or blocked, a tree is going down, she is recognised (deflects, then is quietly pleased), wages are late (blunt, never whiny).
- **Stays quiet when:** she is frightened (works harder instead), and in the cabin late at night.
- **With strange things:** practical denial at first ("Wind in the tops."). If she witnessed something herself, she gets clipped and careful and wants everyone in by dusk.

## EMP-02 Dale Ostrow: Stronger (legacy saves add Skeptical)

- **Age and background:** late 30s. A big, gentle former dockhand who joined for the money.
- **Voice:**
  - Slow, warm and teasing, with long setups and short punchlines.
  - Talks about food constantly.
  - Says "mate" to everyone, including the boss.
- **Habits:**
  - Carries the heavy ones and says so.
  - Narrates the log he is carrying ("This one's got a personality.").
  - Fond of Biscuit, and the dog's main food source.
- **Relationships:**
  - **Marta:** friendly rivalry.
  - **Tomas:** looks out for him.
  - **Joan:** a cards partner who teases him about his cooking.
  - **Priya:** thinks she's the smartest person on the island.
- **Speaks when:** hauling, eating, on breaks, cards and arcade; teasing; and on payday (loud relief).
- **Stays quiet when:** the boss is angry; he is shaken.
- **With strange things:** a skeptic who explains everything (a deer, the fridge, someone's radio). He stays skeptical even about rumors, but firsthand evidence rattles him visibly.

## EMP-03 Priya Lund: Observant, Level-headed

- **Age and background:** around 30. A forestry-surveyor dropout, and methodical.
- **Voice:**
  - Precise and calm, with an exact eye for detail.
  - Asks questions.
  - Understated humour.
  - Says "boss" politely.
- **Habits:**
  - Notices tracks, broken branches, the lean of trees, wind shifts and sounds and where they came from.
  - Keeps a small notebook.
  - Corrects people gently with facts.
- **Relationships:**
  - **Marta:** learning from her.
  - **Joan:** friendly debates about evidence.
  - **Tomas:** reassures him with explanations.
  - **Dale:** amused by him.
- **Speaks when:** she notices something (about the work or otherwise), or someone makes a claim she can check; in reports to the boss (clear, factual).
- **Stays quiet when:** she has no evidence (she won't speculate), and around big group banter.
- **With strange things:** she describes exactly what she perceived: distance, direction, what it wasn't. She is the most credible witness and the hardest to frighten, and recovers quickly (level-headed).

## EMP-04 Tomas Reyner: Timid, Reliable driver

- **Age and background:** mid 20s. First island contract; a former courier driver.
- **Voice:**
  - Polite, hesitant and quick to apologise.
  - Self-deprecating jokes.
  - Says "sorry" and "is that okay?"
  - Calls the boss "boss" carefully.
- **Habits:**
  - Loves the trucks and talks about gears, mud and the route.
  - Asks when dusk is.
  - Wants to head in early.
  - Writes messages home (to his sister, Ana) that he can't send until the boat.
- **Relationships:**
  - **Dale:** a big brother figure.
  - **Marta:** a little scared of her approval.
  - **Priya:** trusts her explanations.
  - **Joan:** she drags him into games.
- **Speaks when:** driving (confident there), dusk approaches (anxious), he is praised (genuinely delighted), and something odd happens (startles, then apologises).
- **Stays quiet when:** the group is arguing, and after he has been frightened.
- **With strange things:** frightens easily and reacts strongly to what he personally witnesses. He is talented and reliable all the same; he is not useless.

## EMP-05 Joan Abernathy: Sociable, Skeptical

- **Age and background:** around 50. A former bar manager, now a camp cook by preference and a logger by contract.
- **Voice:**
  - Warm, chatty and sardonic.
  - Tells stories.
  - Uses nicknames: "love", "sunshine", and "boss-love" for the boss.
- **Habits:**
  - Organises meals, card games and the arcade high-score board.
  - Gossips about the company.
  - Keeps morale up.
  - Asks everyone how they slept.
- **Relationships:** everyone's friend.
  - **Dale:** a cards rival.
  - **Marta:** she teases her.
  - **Tomas:** mothers him.
  - **Priya:** argues with her about "evidence" for fun.
- **Speaks when:** almost always: breaks, meals, walking together, and checking on frightened people.
- **Stays quiet when:** someone is genuinely scared (she listens), and when wages are owed (she goes quieter, worried).
- **With strange things:** skeptical of rumors and online nonsense ("Someone's been reading forums."). She is reassuring, but her reassurance has limits when people she trusts saw something.

## Roles for `speaker: any` / `trait:` lines

Hired replacements and compatible lines use neutral, workmanlike voices. Trait-specific lines must fit the trait: Timid (nervous, wants to head in), Hard-working (keen), Stronger (heavy loads), Level-headed (calm), Sociable (chatty), Skeptical (doubts), Observant (notices), Reliable driver (vehicles).

## Shared crew facts (all may reference)

- **The job:** 30 days to clear the whole island. The company pays $100 a tree, and each worker gets $300 a day.
- **Equipment:** axes (small, medium, large), chainsaws (loud, fast) and gear (boots, gloves, helmets).
- **The logs:** they go to the receiving cradle, then the decks, then the fire pile, and the fire burns slowly.
- **The night:** comes after dusk. The camper and the cabin are where you go at night.
- **Biscuit:** once got lost and was found again (the dog-rescue event; reference it only with `needs`/flags that imply it).
