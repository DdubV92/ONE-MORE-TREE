# Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D | Move |
| Mouse | Look (sensitivity and invert-Y in Settings) |
| Space | Jump |
| Shift | Sprint (forward) |
| Ctrl | Crouch (stays crouched under low ceilings) |
| Left mouse | Swing the axe (hold to keep chopping at the same pace). On a fallen trunk, aim at an orange mark to buck it into logs. Does nothing while carrying. |
| 1 / 2 / 3 | Equip your small / medium / large axe (campaign: only axes you own; Preview: any tier for review) |
| E on a crew member | Talk: what they're doing, their axe, wages, what they noticed, report what you saw, reassure, give orders (follow, work here, head home), hand over a spare axe, pay, fire |
| J | Journal (crew roster and hiring first): contract progress, payroll and arrears (pay employees), money ledger, daily reports |
| F9 / F10 | Quick save / quick load of the current slot (campaign only; a backup of the previous save is kept) |
| E | Interact: doors, fire, the **tool rack** (axe shop), the **camper bed** (sleep: from dusk), notice board, arcade. Logs: pick up / add to your bundle; at the receiving cradle or a log deck: deliver everything you carry (refused, and not paid, if there is no room). Too heavy to lift: hold to drag. Handcart: load logs, grab or let go of the handle, unload at the cradle or a deck. Fire pit: load paid logs onto the fire |
| G | Set down one log at a checked spot in front of you (at the cradle: deliver). Holding paid yard logs: put them on the nearest deck or the fire pile |
| X | Drop the whole load (each log still needs a clear spot; otherwise you keep it) |
| Esc | Pause menu: resume, settings, diagnostics, save (campaign), title, quit. Releases the mouse. |
| Click | Recapture the mouse after using a panel |

## Pickup trucks (Phase 5)

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| E at a truck door | Driver's door: drive. Passenger door: ride. Tailgate: load the logs you carry, or unload (paid at the drop-off by the rack, set down unpaid elsewhere) |
| W / S | Throttle / brake, then reverse once stopped |
| A / D | Steer |
| Space | Parking brake |
| H | Headlights |
| Mouse | Look around the cab |
| E | Get out (only when stopped and the door side is clear and dry) |
| J → Crew | Send a team (driver + two loaders) to log near you, call a team home, recover a stuck truck, order a worker to ride with you |

## Couch and TV (Phase 5)

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| E on the couch | Sit (look around freely) |
| Left click on a tile (or 1-4) | Focus that camera (the one shown at that moment) |
| A / D or mouse wheel | Previous / next camera while focused |
| Right click or Backspace | Back to the four-camera wall |
| C | Channel: camera wall ↔ Timber Tales (entertainment with the wall as an inset) |
| [ / ] | Swap speed: frozen, 5, 10, 20 or 40 s per tile (saved in Settings) |
| M | Mute the camera microphones (the room still sounds normal) |
| - / + | TV volume |
| P | TV power (also E on the TV when standing) |
| E or Space | Stand up |

## Water

Wading is allowed up to about 0.9 m deep. Movement slows as the water gets deeper. Past the limit, the water gently pushes you back toward shallower ground, and a "Too deep to wade" hint appears. Buoys off the landing beach mark the depth. Bridges cross the river at the two trail crossings.

## Character Preview scene

- Drag with the left or right mouse button to orbit.
- Wheel to zoom.
- Middle-drag to pan.
- Esc returns to the title.

## Nights and the hunt (Phase 6)

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| E on the dog during "Find your dog" | Reunite; Biscuit then follows you home |
| E on a hiding place (hunt only) | Hide under a bed, in a closet, under the utility desk, behind the utility shelves, or under the dock |
| E while hidden | Get out (only when the exit is clear) |
| Mouse while hidden | Look around a little |
| E at the end of the dock (day 36) | Board the rescue ship (asks first if crew are still ashore) |

Sleep is replaced by "Find your dog" during the rescue, and is unavailable during the hunt (nights pass on their own).
